How these books work

Every entry is posted as a balanced debit and credit, so the balance sheet always balances and the cash flow always ends at the cash the balance sheet shows. The check rows on each statement should read zero.

What each type does

  • Shareholder capital adds cash and share capital.
  • Internal movement moves cash between Moonie's accounts. Only its fee hits the income statement.
  • Transaction cost is a bank or telco charge, shown above gross profit.
  • Customer deposit adds cash and money owed to the customer. Moonie's fee on it is revenue; the telco's cut is a transaction cost.
  • Customer payout pays customer money out and reduces what Moonie owes them.
  • Expense is an operating cost paid in cash.
  • Investment becomes a fixed asset and is depreciated in equal monthly amounts.

General assumptions

  • Payouts from the disbursement wallet are made for customers and reduce customer money owed. If payouts run ahead of recorded customer deposits, customer money owed goes negative: Moonie has fronted those payouts with its own float.
  • A wallet whose history starts partway through is brought in with an opening balance as customer money.
  • Figures without a statement behind them are marked as estimates until one arrives.
  • Money sent to an unknown destination sits in "In transit" until it is confirmed.
  • Gaps between a provider's reported balance and its transactions are held as unreconciled differences.
  • No accruals: expenses are recorded when paid. No tax is calculated.
